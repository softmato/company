# Softmato Frequently Asked Questions

Headings here are written as the question a visitor actually asks, because
retrieval matches on them. Keep that shape when adding entries.

## Where is Softmato located?

Softmato Technology Pvt Ltd is based in Kathmandu, Nepal. We work with clients
locally and internationally.

## What technology stack does Softmato use?

TypeScript throughout. Next.js and React on the front end, Node.js and
PostgreSQL (with Drizzle ORM) behind it, Tailwind CSS for styling, Cloudflare
R2 for object storage, and native integrations with eSewa, Khalti and Fonepay
for payments.

## How do I book a meeting or consultation?

Ask the assistant for available slots and it will book a 15-minute discovery
call for you. Slots run on weekdays at 10:00, 11:30, 14:00 and 15:30 Nepal time, open
about two weeks ahead, with at least 12 hours notice.

## How do project quotes and estimates work?

Share your requirements and the engineering team reviews the scope, then comes
back with a written proposal covering phases, deliverables, timeline and cost.

Quotes are always specific to a project. The assistant does not quote figures,
day rates or ballpark ranges in chat — a number given without knowing the
scope is worse than no number.

## Can I speak to a person instead of the assistant?

Yes, at any time. Give your name, email and what you would like to discuss and
the assistant passes it to the founders and engineering leads. You can also
use the contact form on the website.

## Does Softmato build mobile apps?

Yes — cross-platform and native applications for iOS and Android, including
payment integration and offline synchronisation.

## Are Nepali payment gateways supported?

Yes. eSewa, Khalti and Fonepay integration is core expertise, backed by
double-entry accounting so payments reconcile against a real ledger rather
than a log of callbacks.

## How long does a project take?

It depends entirely on scope, and the honest answer is that nobody can give
you a timeline before understanding what you want built. The discovery call
exists to get to that answer. The assistant does not estimate durations.

## Who owns the code you write for me?

You do. Once milestone payments are complete, full copyright and intellectual
property rights in the custom software transfer to you.

## Will you sign an NDA?

Yes. Client projects carry NDA provisions by default, and your proprietary
code, business logic, customer records and trade secrets remain your property.

## What happens after launch?

Ongoing maintenance, monitoring and support are covered by a support retainer.
There are Starter, Growth and Enterprise tiers; which one fits depends on the
system and is settled during scoping.

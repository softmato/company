# Softmato Pricing & Project Estimates

Softmato follows a transparent pricing model tailored to project scope, complexity, and ongoing support requirements.

## Project Classification Tiers

### 1. Static Web Tier
- **Best for**: Marketing sites, static landing pages, corporate informational portals.
- **Characteristics**: Content remains static post-launch or is updated infrequently.
- **Includes**: High-fidelity custom designs, responsive layout, SEO metadata, JSON-LD schema, contact form.

### 2. Advanced Web & SaaS Tier
- **Best for**: Dynamic web applications, customer portals, content-managed sites.
- **Characteristics**: Dynamic backend, administrative dashboards, CMS integration, auth systems.
- **Includes**: Custom DB models, admin panel, image upload to Cloudflare R2, role-based auth, email notification systems.

### 3. Custom Enterprise & System Tier
- **Best for**: SaaS products, multi-tenant platforms, complex backend platforms with payment integrations & accounting rules.
- **Characteristics**: Custom business rules, legal compliance, real-time ledger accounting, multi-gateway payments (eSewa/Khalti/Fonepay).
- **Includes**: Monorepo scaffolding, microservices/packages, full testing suite, automated deployment pipelines, dedicated support SLA.

## How Project Quotes Work
1. **Initial Inquiry / Lead Creation**: Create a lead or submit details via our contact form or AI assistant.
2. **Discovery & Architecture Review**: Our technical team assesses technical requirements and defines project scope.
3. **Formal Proposal**: Detailed milestone breakdown, timeline, and fixed quote or milestone-based fee structure.

## Support & Retainer Tiers
Support retainers cover maintenance, infrastructure monitoring, security updates, and priority engineering support:
- **Starter Retainer**: Basic maintenance, uptime monitoring, monthly updates.
- **Growth Retainer**: Dedicated support hours, monthly performance audits, continuous updates.
- **Enterprise Retainer**: 24/7 incident response, custom SLA, dedicated engineering resource allocation.

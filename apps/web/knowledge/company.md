# Softmato Technology Pvt Ltd — Company Overview

## About Softmato

Softmato Technology Pvt Ltd is a premier software engineering and product development firm headquartered in Kathmandu, Nepal. We build high-performance web applications, mobile apps, SaaS products, and custom software systems for startups and enterprises worldwide.

## Equal Founders & Leadership Structure

Softmato Technology is founded and equally led by **Jiwan Mijhar** and **Siddhant Yadav**. Both founders share equal standing, vision, ownership, and leadership authority in the company. They divide their operational responsibilities based on mutual alignment and technical/business specialization:

- **Jiwan Mijhar** (Founder & CEO): Business & Operations Lead — Oversees external operations, strategic growth, legal compliance, client relationships, and corporate partnerships.
- **Siddhant Yadav** (Founder & CTO): Engineering & Product Lead — Oversees software architecture, technical execution, internal product development, system engineering, and technical mapping strategy.

## Core Values & Principles

- **Equal Partnership & Shared Vision**: Softmato operates on strong equal founder synergy where technical engineering and business execution move hand-in-hand.
- **Engineering Excellence**: We write robust, type-safe, and maintainable software using modern paradigms.
- **Data Integrity & Security**: Critical money and business operations are backed by immutable double-entry ledger logic and strict database constraints.
- **Production Readiness**: Every application we release undergoes rigorous automated testing, accessibility audits, and performance tuning.
- **Transparency**: Clear, predictable pricing tiers and open communication at every stage of product development.

## Core Technology Stack

- **Frontend & Web**: Next.js (App Router), React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Three.js / WebGL micro-animations.
- **Backend & Database**: Node.js, PostgreSQL (Neon / Docker), Drizzle ORM, REST & GraphQL APIs.
- **Storage & Cloud**: Cloudflare R2, Vercel, Docker.
- **Payments & Accounting**: Native integration with eSewa, Khalti, Fonepay, and automated double-entry accounting engines.

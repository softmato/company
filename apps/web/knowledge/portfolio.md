# Softmato Portfolio & Case Studies

Softmato Technology Pvt Ltd has built and delivered high-impact software systems across diverse industries:

## 1. Softmato Platform

- **Type**: Enterprise SaaS, Centralized Payments & Accounting Platform.
- **Description**: A multi-surface Next.js platform integrating public marketing, founder-only administrative management, centralized payment orchestration (Khalti, eSewa, Fonepay), and double-entry accounting with PostgreSQL enforcement.
- **Key Features**: Micro-animations with Three.js, gapless fiscal period tracking, R2 storage, Auth.js TOTP security.

## 2. Product Engineering & SaaS Solutions

- High-concurrency web and mobile applications engineered for security, zero-downtime deployments, and real-time state management.
- Multi-tenant client portals with presigned document access and project delivery trackers.

## 3. Custom Mobile & Web Applications

- Customized web apps for fintech, agency clients, and operational systems across Nepal and international markets.
- Tailored payment SDKs enabling third-party SaaS products to process wallet transactions without writing native payment driver code.

## Technology & Engineering Highlights

- **100% Type Safety**: End-to-end TypeScript monorepos with Turbo & Pnpm.
- **Accessibility & SEO**: 100/100 Lighthouse accessibility compliance across public web surfaces.
- **Immutable Ledger Technology**: Financial transactions enforced via PostgreSQL DDL constraints and deferred triggers.

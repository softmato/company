# Softmato Services & Technical Offerings

Softmato Technology Pvt Ltd provides end-to-end software development services across four primary pillars:

## 1. Product Engineering

We transform product ideas into scalable, production-ready SaaS platforms and enterprise applications.

- **Architecture Design**: Event-driven systems, domain-driven design, and monorepo scaffolding.
- **Full-Stack Development**: Next.js, React, Node.js, and Cloud-native infrastructure.
- **Database & Ledger Systems**: High-reliability transaction engines, PostgreSQL DDL design, double-entry accounting engines.
- **Testing & Quality Assurance**: Comprehensive automated test suites (Vitest, Playwright, ESLint, TypeScript).

## 2. Web Applications

Custom web applications built with speed, accessibility, and high visual polish.

- **Modern UI/UX**: Motion primitives, WebGL/Three.js visual features, responsive glassmorphism and modern dark/light mode palettes.
- **SEO & Performance**: 100/100 accessibility standards, sub-second load times, structured JSON-LD schema, canonical SEO optimization.
- **CMS Integration**: Custom database-backed Content Management Systems with Cloudflare R2 media management.

## 3. Mobile Apps

Cross-platform and native mobile application engineering.

- **iOS & Android**: High-performance mobile clients with offline support, local caching, and real-time synchronization.
- **Payment & Wallet Integrations**: In-app purchases, eSewa, Khalti, and Fonepay integrations.
- **Push Notifications & Analytics**: Secure messaging pipelines and user event tracking.

## 4. Technical Consulting & Infrastructure

- **Security & Compliance**: Database security, TOTP/MFA, argon2id encryption at rest, rate-limiting, and legal compliance checks.
- **Payment Gateway Architecture**: Multi-provider payment orchestration, reconciliation engines, and webhook pipelines.

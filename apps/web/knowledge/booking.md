# Booking a Discovery Call with Softmato

How booking a call with Softmato works. Availability, timings and
confirmation details are exactly as described here — nothing beyond this
should be stated as fact.

## What a discovery call is

A short introductory call with the Softmato engineering team to understand
what you want to build. It is a scoping conversation, not a sales pitch: you
describe the problem, we ask questions, and you leave with a clearer picture
of shape, approach and next steps.

- **Length**: about 15 minutes.
- **Cost**: free. There is no charge for a discovery call.
- **Who joins**: a member of the Softmato engineering team.

## When calls are available

- **Days**: weekdays only. Saturdays and Sundays are not offered.
- **Times**: 10:00, 11:30, 14:00 and 15:30, Nepal Standard Time (NPT, UTC+05:45).
- **How far ahead**: slots are open for roughly the next two weeks.
- **Shortest notice**: a slot must be at least 12 hours away, so same-day
  bookings late in the day are not possible.

Times are always quoted in Nepal Standard Time. If you are joining from
another timezone, say so and the team will confirm the local equivalent with
you.

## How to book

1. Ask the assistant what slots are open. It lists the next available times.
2. Give your **name**, your **email**, and the **slot** you want.
3. The assistant confirms the booking and gives you a reference code that
   looks like `BK-XKCDF29`.
4. A confirmation email goes to the address you gave, and the Softmato team is
   alerted at the same time.

All three details are required. The assistant will ask for anything missing
rather than guessing — it will not book a call under a name or address you did
not give it.

## Dates in confirmations

Dates are shown Bikram Sambat first with the Gregorian date in brackets — for
example `17 Bhadra 2082 (2 Sep 2026)`. This matches how dates appear
everywhere else on Softmato invoices and records.

## Changing or cancelling a booking

Reply to the confirmation email, quoting your reference code, and the team
will move or cancel the slot. The assistant itself cannot yet reschedule or
cancel an existing booking.

## If a slot is not available

A slot already taken, or one outside the times above, will be refused and the
assistant will offer the alternatives that are genuinely open. A booking is
only confirmed when the slot is actually held.

## If you would rather not book a call

You do not have to. You can instead share your project requirements and have
the team come back to you by email, or ask to be put in touch with a person
directly.

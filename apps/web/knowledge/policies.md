# Softmato Business Policies & Governance

Softmato Technology Pvt Ltd maintains strict policies to ensure client confidentiality, data privacy, and legal compliance.

## 1. Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure (NDA)
- Every client project is protected by default NDA provisions.
- Proprietary code, business logic, customer records, and trade secrets remain 100% client property.

## 2. Security & Data Protection
- **Data Encryption**: All database passwords, TOTP secrets, and API credentials are encrypted at rest.
- **Access Control**: Administrative operations require multi-factor authentication (TOTP) and session validation.
- **Media & Asset Safety**: Assets stored in Cloudflare R2 follow strict public/private bucket segregation.

## 3. Code Ownership & IP Transfer
- Upon completion of milestone payments, full copyright and Intellectual Property (IP) rights for custom software developed for a client are transferred to the client.

## 4. Refund & Cancellation Policy
- Refund parameters and project cancellation clauses are defined in individual service contracts and statements of work (SOW).
- Payments processed through gateways are subject to audit and verification before settlement.

## 5. Support & Maintenance SLA
- SLA response targets are governed by active support retainer contracts.
- Critical security vulnerabilities and system downtime are prioritized with emergency engineering response.

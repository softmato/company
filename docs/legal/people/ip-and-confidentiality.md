## 1. What this covers

"**Work Product**" means anything you create, alone or with others, in the
course of working with us or using our time, equipment, code, accounts, or
confidential information: source code, designs, documentation, schemas,
scripts, prompts, models, illustrations, copy, test suites, and the ideas and
inventions behind them.

"**Confidential Information**" means anything non-public you learn through us:
our source code and architecture, unreleased products, client lists and client
work, commercial terms, pricing, credentials and keys, financial information,
and personal information about colleagues, candidates, clients, or their users.
It does not stop being confidential because you can remember it without notes.

## 2. Work Product is ours

You assign to the Company, absolutely and for the full term of protection
worldwide, all rights in the Work Product — copyright under the **Copyright
Act, 2059**, and any patent, design, database, or other right that arises. The
assignment takes effect as each item is created, not when this is signed and
not when you are paid.

Where a right cannot be assigned under Nepali law, you grant us an exclusive,
irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free licence to it, and you waive any moral
right to object to how the work is modified or used, to the extent the law
allows a waiver.

You will, on request and at our cost, sign whatever else is needed to register
or defend these rights, including after you have left.

## 3. What stays yours

**Anything you made before joining us, and anything you make entirely on your
own time**, using none of our equipment, code, accounts, or Confidential
Information, and unrelated to our business or to anything you work on here.
That work is yours and this agreement does not touch it.

If you want to be certain a specific project stays yours, list it here before
signing. Anything not listed is presumed covered by section 2, so list it.

**Your open-source contributions.** You may contribute to open-source projects

**Your portfolio.** You may say you worked here and describe in general terms
what you built. You may not publish our source code, our clients' names where
the client asked us not to, screenshots of unreleased work, or anything covered
by section 4.

## 4. Confidentiality

You will keep Confidential Information secret, use it only to do your work
here, and not copy it anywhere we have not approved — personal laptops,
personal cloud accounts, personal repositories, or messaging apps outside the
tools we issue.

This applies with equal force to **our clients' information**, which is often
more sensitive than our own. A client's production data, credentials, customer
records, and commercial terms are theirs, and we hold them under obligations of
our own.

It does not apply to information that is already public through no fault of
yours, that you already lawfully had, or that you are required by law or a
court to disclose — and if that last one happens, tell us first if you are
allowed to.

**This survives the end of your time here indefinitely.** It is not limited to
a period of years, because confidential information does not become harmless on
a schedule.

## 5. Third-party material and AI tools

Do not put anything into our products that you do not have the right to put
there: copied code, licensed assets, or content taken from a previous employer.
If you bring code under an open-source licence, tell us which licence, because
some of them attach conditions to everything they touch.

Where you use an AI coding or content tool, the output is Work Product under
section 2 like anything else. Do not paste Confidential Information, client
data, credentials, or personal information into a tool we have not approved —
see `it-and-device-policy.md` for the approved list.

## 6. Personal information

Personal information you handle here — about colleagues, candidates, clients,
or their end users — is not yours to keep, copy, or take with you. Handle it
under the **Individual Privacy Act, 2075** and our Privacy Policy, and only as
far as your work needs.

## 7. When you leave

On or before your last day you will return every device, document, and copy,
and delete any of our material from personal devices and accounts. You will not
retain source code, client work, or Confidential Information in any form. See
`offboarding-checklist.md`.

Sections 2, 4, 6 and 7 survive the end of this agreement.

## 8. Not a restraint on your career

This agreement is about our information and our work product. **It does not
stop you working anywhere you like afterwards**, and it does not stop you using
the general skill and experience you gained here. What it stops is taking our
code, our confidential information, and our clients' information with you.

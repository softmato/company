## 1. What this is, and what it is not

This is a **training placement**. Its purpose is that you learn to work as a
software professional by doing real work with supervision.

**It is not employment.** You are not our employee, this is not a job offer,
and completing it does not entitle you to one. You are not on our payroll, we
do not deduct social security contributions for you, and the leave and notice
rules in the employee handbook do not apply to you.

We take that distinction seriously in practice as well as on paper: you are
supervised, your work is reviewed for your benefit as well as ours, and **you
are not used to fill a role we would otherwise pay someone to do**.

## 4. Money

**Track A — stipend.** We pay you a stipend of **NPR [confirm: amount] per
[month / the placement]**, paid **[confirm: monthly, by which date]** by bank
transfer. A stipend is income and tax is deducted where the **Income Tax Act,
2058** requires it. It is a stipend supporting your training, not a wage, and
it does not make this employment.

**Track B — unpaid.** This placement is unpaid. In exchange, its purpose is
your learning: you will be supervised, given work chosen for what it teaches
you, and never left to do routine production work simply because it needs
doing. If that stops being true, tell us — we will either fix it or move you to
Track A.

## 6. What you create belongs to the Company

You sign `ip-and-confidentiality.md` with this agreement. Work you do here
belongs to the Company — including on Track B, where the absence of payment
changes nothing about ownership. Anything you built before this placement, or
build on your own time and equipment unconnected to us, stays yours; list it in
that document.

**You keep the right to talk about it.** You may put the placement on your CV,
say what you worked on in general terms, and describe what you learned. You may
not publish our source code, our clients' names, or anything not yet released.
If you want to show something specific in a portfolio, ask — we will usually
say yes.

## 7. Confidentiality

Everything you see here that is not public — our code, our clients, their data,
commercial terms, credentials — stays confidential, during the placement and
afterwards, indefinitely. Client information deserves particular care: it is
not ours to be careless with either.

## 8. Conduct and safety

`anti-harassment-policy.md` protects you and binds you. **You are covered by it
in full from your first day, on every track**, and you may use its complaint
route without going through your supervisor.

You are expected to be honest about what you have and have not done, to ask
when stuck rather than guess in production, and to treat colleagues and clients
with respect.

## 9. Ending it early

Either of us may end the placement on **[confirm: 7 days]** written notice, and
you may leave sooner if you need to — this is training, not a bond, and nothing
holds you here. We may end it immediately for serious misconduct or a breach of
`ip-and-confidentiality.md`.

**Track C:** if the placement ends early, we tell your institution, say why
factually, and refund the unused part of the training fee to them as that
agreement sets out.

## 10. Certificate

On finishing the placement and meeting the objectives in section 3, you get a
**certificate of completion** stating the dates, what you worked on, and what
you were assessed on.

The certificate is earned, not automatic. If you do not complete the placement
or do not meet the objectives, we say so honestly — to you first, and to your
institution on Track C. We will not certify something that did not happen.

There is **no charge** for the certificate on any track.

## 11. Reference

We are glad to give a reference afterwards and will give an honest one. Ask
your supervisor.

## 12. General

Governed by the laws of Nepal. Courts at **Kathmandu** have jurisdiction. This
agreement, with the documents named in section 6 and 8, is the whole
arrangement between us.

training fee** for supervising them. That makes the institute a paying customer
and this a commercial contract, so it sits under the Company's public **Terms
of Service** like any other engagement.

Each student also signs `internship-agreement.md` (Track C). This document does
not bind the student and that one does not bind the institute.

## 1. What we provide

A supervised practical training placement in software development for the
students named in Schedule A, running from **[start]** to **[end]**.

For each student we provide: a named supervisor, real project work chosen for
what it teaches, **[confirm: hours per week]** of supervised work, regular
one-to-one review, a written assessment at the end, and a certificate of
completion where it is earned.

**What this is not.** It is not employment, not a job guarantee, and not a
promise that any student will be hired. We will not tell a student otherwise
and we ask that the Institute does not either.

## 2. Students

Listed in **Schedule A**, with each student's name, course, contact details,
and the objectives agreed for their placement. Numbers may change only by
written agreement — a student cannot be substituted after the placement starts
without our agreement, because supervision is planned per person.

**Capacity is finite.** We take at most **[confirm: number]** students in a
cohort, because supervision that is spread thinner than that stops being
training.

## 4. What the Institute provides

- Confirmation that each student is enrolled and is at least 18, or a parent's
  or guardian's consent where they are not.
- A named academic supervisor we can reach during the placement.
- Any accessibility or medical need we should know about to supervise safely,
  with the student's consent.
- Prompt notice if a student withdraws from the course.

## 5. Reporting and certificates

At the end of the placement, for each student, we give the Institute: the dates
attended, what they worked on, an assessment against the agreed objectives, and
whether the certificate was earned.

**We report honestly.** If a student did not attend, did not complete, or did
not meet the objectives, that is what the report says. We will not issue a
certificate for a placement that did not happen, and the fee does not purchase
one — it purchases the supervision.

Interim concerns — attendance, conduct, a student struggling — are raised with
the Institute's supervisor as they arise, not saved for the final report.

## 6. Information about students

Each of us handles student information under the **Individual Privacy Act,
2075 (2018)**.

- The Institute may pass us only what the placement needs, and only with the
  student's knowledge.
- We report back only what section 5 describes. **Nothing else** — not
  personal messages, not health information, not anything unrelated to the
  placement.
- We keep placement records for **[confirm: 24 months]** after the placement
  ends, then delete them.
- Neither of us passes student information to anyone else without the student's
  consent, unless the law requires it.

Our Candidate Privacy Notice explains this to students directly, and we give it
to each of them at the start.

## 7. Conduct and safety

Our `anti-harassment-policy.md` covers every student on placement from their
first day, with a complaint route that does not run through their supervisor.
We will tell the Institute promptly about any incident involving one of its
students, subject to the student's own confidentiality where it is their
complaint to make.

We may end a placement immediately for serious misconduct, having told the
Institute why.

## 8. What students create

Work a student produces during the placement belongs to Softmato, under the
agreement each student signs. Students keep the right to describe the placement
and what they learned, and to show agreed work in a portfolio.

Our teaching material, code, and templates remain ours. The Institute may not
reproduce them in its own courses without our written agreement.

## 9. Cancellation

- **The Institute cancels before the placement starts:** more than
  **[confirm: 14 days]** ahead, the fee is refunded in full; inside that, we
  keep **[confirm: 50%]**, because the supervision slot cannot be refilled at
  short notice.
- **A student withdraws part-way:** we refund the unused portion of that
  student's fee, counted in whole weeks.
- **We cannot deliver:** we refund the full fee for anything not delivered.

Refunds follow our public **Refund & Cancellation Policy** where this section
is silent.

## 10. Liability

Each of us is responsible for its own negligence. Our total liability under
this agreement is limited to the fees paid under it. Neither of us is liable
for indirect or consequential loss.

**We do not guarantee any employment outcome**, and no part of the fee is
referable to one.

## 11. Term and termination

This agreement runs for the cohort in Schedule A. Either of us may end it on
**30 days** written notice, or immediately for a material breach not cured
within 15 days. Placements already running continue to their end unless the
breach makes that impossible.

## 12. General

Governed by the laws of Nepal; courts at **Kathmandu** have jurisdiction.
Neither of us may use the other's name or logo in marketing without written
consent, though each may state factually that the arrangement exists.

This agreement and the Company's Terms of Service are the whole agreement
between us. Where they conflict on the subject of this placement, this
agreement governs.

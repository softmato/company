# Offboarding Checklist

> Fill every `[confirm: …]` before first use.

Run for **everyone** who leaves — employee, intern on any track, contractor,
founder. Copy it per person and keep the completed copy in their personnel
record.

The point is not tidiness. An account nobody closed is how a breach happens six
months after someone left, and an unpaid statutory due is how a clean departure
becomes a labour claim.

## 1. Before the last day

- [ ] Resignation or termination acknowledged **in writing**, stating the last
      working day
- [ ] Notice period confirmed against the appointment letter and the Labour
      Act, and any pay in lieu agreed in writing
- [ ] Handover document written — what they own, where it is, what is half
      finished, what is about to break
- [ ] Handover walkthrough done with the person taking over
- [ ] Client and supplier contacts introduced to their replacement
- [ ] Anything only they can do is documented or transferred (deploy access,
      provider dashboards, domain registrar, bank tokens)
- [ ] Outstanding expenses submitted
- [ ] Remaining leave calculated — taken or paid out
- [ ] Exit conversation held, and what was said written down

## 2. On the last day — access

Do these **on the day, not the week after.**

- [ ] Email account suspended, forwarding set to [confirm: who] for
      [confirm: 90 days]
- [ ] Code host access removed (repositories, org membership, deploy keys,
      personal access tokens)
- [ ] Cloud and hosting console access removed
- [ ] Database access removed, and any personal credentials dropped
- [ ] Admin panel account disabled — **disabled, not deleted**, so the audit
      log keeps its history
- [ ] Payment provider dashboard access removed (eSewa, Khalti, Fonepay, banks)
- [ ] Password manager access revoked, and **every shared secret they could see
      rotated** — not just their own
- [ ] Any API keys or tokens they created rotated
- [ ] Chat, project tracker, design tools, analytics access removed
- [ ] Two-factor devices deregistered
- [ ] VPN and any SSH keys removed
- [ ] Removed from mailing lists, on-call rota, and shared calendars
- [ ] Physical: office keys, access card, anything else that opens a door

**Where the departure is not amicable, revoke access first and hold the
conversation afterwards.** That is not distrust; it is the standard practice
that keeps a bad day from becoming an incident.

## 3. Devices and data

- [ ] Laptop, phone, monitor, peripherals returned and logged
- [ ] Device wiped and re-imaged before reissue
- [ ] Company data deleted from their personal devices and cloud accounts,
      **confirmed in writing by them**
- [ ] Local copies of client work and production data confirmed deleted
- [ ] Anything on their machine that exists nowhere else identified and moved
      before the wipe

## 4. Money and statutory

- [ ] Final salary or stipend calculated to the last working day
- [ ] Leave encashment calculated
- [ ] Pay in lieu of notice, where applicable
- [ ] Gratuity and any other statutory dues under the **Labour Act, 2074**
- [ ] Final **Social Security Fund** contribution filed
- [ ] **TDS** deducted and deposited; annual statement issued
- [ ] Bonus due under the **Bonus Act, 2030**, where applicable
- [ ] Expense claims settled
- [ ] Any company loan or advance settled
- [ ] Final settlement paid **[confirm: within how many days of the last
      working day]**, and a written statement given showing how it was
      calculated

## 5. Paperwork

- [ ] Experience or service letter issued, on request
- [ ] **Intern:** certificate of completion issued where earned, and the
      assessment written
- [ ] **Track C intern:** final report sent to the institution, and any unused
      training fee refunded
- [ ] Confidentiality and IP obligations confirmed in writing as continuing —
      they survive the end of employment indefinitely
- [ ] Personnel record closed and retained under the retention rule in the
      Privacy Policy
- [ ] Reference contact person agreed, so future requests do not go astray

## 6. The team and the outside world

- [ ] Team told, before they hear it sideways
- [ ] Clients they worked with told, with a named replacement
- [ ] Website team page, email signatures, and any public listing updated
- [ ] Shared documents they own transferred to a new owner
- [ ] Rota and recurring meetings updated

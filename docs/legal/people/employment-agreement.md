# Employment Agreement / Appointment Letter

> **Draft — not reviewed by a lawyer.** Fill every `[confirm: …]` before use.

For **payroll employees** — developers, managers, and anyone else on staff. The
**Labour Act, 2074 (2017)** requires a written appointment letter, so this is
not optional paperwork: an employee working without one is a compliance
failure from their first day.

Give it with `handbook.md`, `ip-and-confidentiality.md`,
`anti-harassment-policy.md`, and `it-and-device-policy.md`.

## 2. Probation

Probation runs for **[confirm: 3 months — the Labour Act permits up to 6;
choose and state one]** from your start date. During it, either of us may end
the employment on **[confirm: 14 days]** notice without giving a reason.

We will tell you in writing before probation ends whether you are confirmed. If
we say nothing and you keep working, you are confirmed.

## 3. Hours and rest

Normal hours are **8 per day and 48 per week**, worked **[confirm: Sunday to
Friday]**, with **Saturday** as the weekly day of rest. Where the Labour Act,
2074 or the Labour Rules give you more rest or shorter hours than this clause
does, **the Act applies**.

We do not expect or reward routine overtime. Where extra hours are genuinely
required and we ask for them, they are paid at the statutory overtime rate, or
taken back as time off if you prefer.

If you are on a support rota for a hosted product, the rota, its rotation, and
how on-call time is compensated are set out in the handbook. **On-call is
compensated; it is never an unpaid expectation.**

## 6. Leave

Your leave entitlements are in `handbook.md` and form part of this agreement.
They cover public holidays, home leave, sick leave, mourning leave, and
maternity and paternity leave.

**Where the Labour Act, 2074 or the Labour Rules, 2075 entitle you to more
leave than the handbook grants, the Act applies.** The handbook sets our
policy; it can be more generous than the law and never less.

## 7. Confidentiality, intellectual property, and conduct

You sign `ip-and-confidentiality.md` alongside this agreement. Everything you
create here belongs to the Company, and our information and our clients'
information stay confidential — indefinitely, including after you leave.

`anti-harassment-policy.md` and `it-and-device-policy.md` also apply to you and
form part of the terms of your employment.

**Outside work.** You may take other paid work or run something of your own,
provided it does not compete with us, does not use our time, equipment or
information, and does not stop you doing this job. Tell us first so we can
confirm there is no conflict. We will not withhold that unreasonably.

## 8. Ending the employment

After probation, either of us may end the employment on **[confirm: 30 days]**
written notice, or by paying salary in lieu of it. Where the Labour Act
requires a longer notice period for your length of service, that period
applies.

We may dismiss without notice for serious misconduct — theft, fraud, violence,
a serious breach of `ip-and-confidentiality.md`, or conduct of comparable
gravity. Before we do, you will be told what is alleged, given the evidence,
and given a real chance to answer it, as the Labour Act requires. Dismissal is
a decision taken after that hearing, never before it.

On leaving, you work through `offboarding-checklist.md`: devices and access
returned, final settlement paid, and any statutory dues cleared.

## 9. Grievances

If something is wrong, raise it with your manager. If it concerns your manager,
or is not resolved, raise it with **[confirm: name of founder or grievance
contact]**. Complaints of harassment go through `anti-harassment-policy.md`
instead, which has its own route and does not require you to speak to your
manager first.

## 10. General

Governed by the laws of Nepal, including the Labour Act, 2074. Courts at
**Kathmandu** have jurisdiction. Where any clause here gives you less than
Nepali law requires, the law applies and the clause is read down to meet it —
the rest of the agreement stands.

This letter, the handbook, and the documents named in section 7 are the whole
agreement between us.

> **Draft — not reviewed by a lawyer.** Fill every `[confirm: …]` before use.

Forms part of the employment agreement. Applies to **payroll employees**;
interns are covered by `internship-agreement.md` instead, except for the
conduct and safety sections, which apply to everyone.

**The standing rule for this whole document:** where the **Labour Act, 2074
(2017)**, the **Labour Rules, 2075**, or any other prevailing law gives you
more than a clause here does, **the law applies and the clause yields**. This
handbook sets policy that may be more generous than the law and is never
allowed to be less. That is why the leave counts below are stated as our policy
rather than as a summary of the Act — check the Act for the statutory floor.

## 3. Probation and confirmation

Probation runs for the period in your appointment letter — **at most 6 months**,
as the Labour Act allows. You get feedback at the halfway point and a written
decision before it ends. We will not extend probation instead of making a
decision.

## 4. Pay

Paid monthly by bank transfer by **[confirm: date]**, with a payslip showing
gross, deductions and net. TDS is deducted and deposited under the **Income Tax
Act, 2058**, and you get an annual statement for your own return.

Social Security Fund contributions are handled as set out in your appointment
letter. Bonus, once we are profitable, follows the **Bonus Act, 2030**.

**Salary is confidential to you.** You are free to discuss your own pay with
anyone, including colleagues — we will not treat that as misconduct. What you
may not do is disclose someone else's.

## 7. How we work

- **Write things down.** Decisions in issues and commits, not in chat that
  scrolls away.
- **Ask early.** Guessing in production is worse than asking a question.
- **Review kindly.** Comment on the code, never the person.
- **Own the mistake.** Say it fast so it can be fixed. Nobody is punished for a
  mistake owned quickly and honestly; hiding one is the misconduct.
- **Money code gets extra care.** See `docs/RULES.md` — those rules are not
  suggestions.

## 8. Conduct

Be honest, be respectful, and do not put the company or a client at risk.

`anti-harassment-policy.md` and `it-and-device-policy.md` apply in full and
form part of your terms of employment.

**Conflicts of interest.** Tell us if you have an interest in a supplier, a
client, or a competitor, or if a family member does. Declaring it is almost
always the end of the matter; not declaring it is the problem.

**Outside work** is fine where it does not compete with us, does not use our
time, equipment or information, and does not stop you doing this job. Tell us
first.

**Gifts.** Anything beyond token hospitality from a client or supplier, tell
your manager. Never accept anything in exchange for a decision.

## 9. Performance and discipline

Feedback happens continuously and formally **[confirm: how often]**. Nothing in
a formal review should ever be a surprise.

Where performance or conduct is a problem, we say so early and specifically,
agree what needs to change and by when, and give real support to change it.
Where it does not improve, or where the conduct is serious, we follow the
process in your appointment letter — you are told what is alleged, shown the
evidence, and given a genuine chance to answer before any decision. **That
hearing is not a formality**, and it is what the Labour Act requires.

## 10. Raising a problem

Talk to your manager first. If it concerns your manager, or goes unresolved,
go to **[confirm: founder / grievance contact]**. We will respond within
**[confirm: 5 working days]**.

Harassment complaints go through `anti-harassment-policy.md`, which has its own
route and does not require you to speak to your manager at all.

## 11. Leaving

Notice periods are in your appointment letter. On your way out we work through
`offboarding-checklist.md` — access, devices, handover, and final settlement.

We will give an honest reference. Leaving well matters more than leaving
quietly; tell us why you are going and we will listen without holding it
against you.
